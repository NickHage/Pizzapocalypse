What is Pizza Technically?

Wikipedia describes pizza as:

"A dish of Italian origin consisting of a usually round, flat base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and often various other ingredients (such as various types of sausage, anchovies, mushrooms, onions, olives, vegetables, meat, ham, etc.), which is then baked at a high temperature, traditionally in a wood-fired oven."

A Quick Origin of Pizza

The roots of pizza has been around for thousands of years, startting with flatbreads with the ancient Egyptians, Greeks, and Romans. The more modern idea of Pizza started in Naples Italy during the 17th century. Then in the late 19th centure, Italian immigrants brough pizza to the United States.
