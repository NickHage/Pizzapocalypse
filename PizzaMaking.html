Crust

The crust is an important part of the pizza since it is the base and is in almost every bite.

  • Thin: A Thinner crispy-ier crust.
  • Deep: Like a pizza version of a bread bowl.
  • Regular: Your regular crust.
  • Chicago Deep: Like regular deep but things are added in reverse. (i.e. chesse, toppings, sauce.)
  • Detroit: A big deep square.
  • Colorado: Thick braided crust that is sweetened with honey.

Sauce

The sauce is what adds additional flavors.

  • Marinara Sauce: Classic red.
  • Pesto: Green and herby.
  • Buffalo: For a good kick.
  • White Garlic: Do not eat on dates.
  • Olive Oil with Herbes: For a little extra flavor without the sauce.

Cheese

All cheeses are great, but some are better.

  • Cheddar: A classic.
  • Mozzarella: For that string.
  • Provolone: Flavor boost.
  • Blue: For extra funk?
  • Smoked Gouda: For some smokey richness

Toppings

The toppings are big for additional flavors and textures.

  • Pepporoni: A classic meat.
  • Pineapple: For those who want to join a war.
  • Anchovies: For salt?
  • Anything you want would work.

Cooking

How you cook your pizza drastically changes the flavor and texture.

  • Oven: The usual way.
  • Wood Burning Oven: Arguably the best way, adds a lot of flavor.
  • Microwave: Good if the pizza has already been baked.
  • Fire pit: Sure, if you can do it.
